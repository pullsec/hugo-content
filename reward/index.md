## FAQ

{{< admonition type=faqs title="Q: How can I support this project?" open=false >}}

**A**: The simplest way is just to use it. Reading articles, sharing them, or reporting mistakes already helps more than you might think. If you want to go further, you can use the support options above.

Nothing is expected — the project stays open either way.

{{< /admonition >}}

{{< admonition type=faqs title="Q: Are the articles actually tested?" open=false >}}

**A**: Yes — or at least that is always the goal. Most content is based on lab environments where things are tested, broken, and rebuilt until they make sense.

When something cannot be fully reproduced, it is based on public research and clearly stated.
The goal is simple: avoid writing about things that are not understood or validated.

{{< /admonition >}}

{{< admonition type=faqs title="Q: Why no exploit code or full PoCs?" open=false >}}

**A**: Because that’s not the goal here, the focus is on understanding:

- how vulnerabilities work
- what primitives they expose
- how they can realistically be abused

Not on providing ready-to-use exploitation, clear explanations are more useful long-term than copy-paste exploits.

{{< /admonition >}}
